Short version: nothing is tracked unless you say yes.

## Analytics

This blog and [wstech.tech](https://wstech.tech) use **Google Analytics 4** to count which pages people read, roughly where readers come from, and what device they use. It is **only loaded after you click "Accept"** on the cookie banner. If you decline, or never answer, no Google script runs at all.

When it runs, Google sets its own `_ga` cookies and processes your IP address to estimate location. I see aggregated reports only, never who you are.

## Your choice

Your answer is stored in a cookie called `wstech_consent` on `.wstech.tech` for one year, so it applies to both sites. You can change it at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer, or by clearing cookies for this site.

## Comments

Comments under posts use [giscus](https://giscus.app), which stores them as GitHub Discussions. The comment box loads from giscus.app when you scroll to it. Posting requires signing in with GitHub, and what you post is public on GitHub.
